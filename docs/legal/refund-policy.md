**14 days, no questions asked.** If you bought something in Lucky Cat HSK and
you would rather not have, email support@luckycathsk.com within 14 days of the
purchase and we will refund it in full. You do not need to give a reason, and we
will not ask you for one.

## 1. What this covers

| Purchase | Price | Refundable |

| Supporter unlock (one-time) | ฿79 | Yes — 14 days, no questions asked |
| Coin packs | from ฿29 | Yes — 14 days, no questions asked |

The app itself is free. There is no subscription, nothing recurring, and nothing
that renews — so there is never anything to cancel.

## 2. How to request a refund

Email **support@luckycathsk.com** from any address and tell us enough to find
the purchase. The most useful details are:

- roughly when you bought it, and which item;
- the email address or name you used at checkout; and
- if you have it, the receipt Stripe emailed you.

If you cannot find any of that, write to us anyway — we would rather find the
purchase ourselves than turn you away over a missing receipt.

## 3. How long it takes

- We aim to reply within **2 business days**.
- Once approved, we issue the refund to the **original payment method**
  immediately.
- How fast the money actually lands is up to your bank or payment provider.
  Cards typically take **5–10 business days**. **PromptPay** refunds are
  returned to the bank account that paid and can take several business days.

## 4. What happens to what you bought

When a purchase is refunded, the thing it bought is removed: the Supporter
status is withdrawn, and the coins it granted are deducted from your balance. If
you have already spent some of those coins, your balance can go to zero but we
do not take away items you have already unlocked with them, and we do not ask
you for the difference.

Refunding does not delete your account or your learning progress. Your streak,
mastered words, and study history are untouched.

## 5. If you bought inside the Android app

Purchases made through the Google Play version are taken by **Google**, not by
us, and Google's own refund process applies to them — Play Store → your order →
request a refund. Google generally handles the first 48 hours directly. After
that, or if Google declines and you are still within 14 days of buying, email us
and we will help.

Purchases made on the web at luckycathsk.com are taken by **Stripe** on our
behalf, and we refund those ourselves under this policy.

## 6. Beyond 14 days

We are not strict about the deadline where something clearly went wrong — a
double charge, a purchase that never arrived, a payment you did not recognise or
authorise. Contact us whenever you notice it and we will put it right,
regardless of how long it has been.

## 7. Your statutory rights

This policy is offered in addition to your legal rights, and takes nothing away
from them. If you are a consumer in Thailand, your rights under Thai consumer
protection law apply. If you are in the EU or UK, your statutory right to
withdraw from a distance contract for digital content applies as well.

If you disagree with a decision we have made, reply and say so — a person reads
every message, and we would much rather resolve it directly than have you raise
a dispute with your bank.
